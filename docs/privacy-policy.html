Jjak (“the app”) is a puzzle game. We built it to work without an account and to keep as little information about you as possible. This policy explains what happens to data when you play.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, petals, album, daily results and settings) and the birth year you enter on first launch are saved only on your device. We do not have servers and we never receive this information. If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your phone, Google may back up this game data to your Google account under Android’s own backup terms.

We ask for birth year only to keep advertising appropriate for your age. The year itself never leaves your device; the app sends ad requests flagged as, for example, “child-directed” or “under the age of consent” where that applies.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the Android advertising IDAd delivery, measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location derived from IP addressAd delivery, fraud prevention
App interactions with ads, diagnostics and crash dataMeasurement, analytics, fraud prevention

For players who indicate they are under 13, ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment: ads are limited to general-audience content, are not personalised, and the advertising ID is not sent, in line with Google Play’s Families policy and COPPA.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or a US state with privacy laws, you will be asked for consent through Google’s consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Sharing

When you tap “Share result”, the app passes a short text (your daily time and score) to your device’s share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

4. Children

Jjak is suitable for players aged 10 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents with questions can contact us at the address above.

5. Your choices and deleting data

  • Use Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app, to delete all game data stored on your device.
  • Use Settings → Ad privacy choices (where shown) to change ad consent.

6. Security

Data handled by Google’s ad services is encrypted in transit. Game data stays on your device.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app’s release notes.
